Meet Anchala

Why I do this work

I'm a corporate trainer with over a decade of experience in Learning & Development — a Master's degree, TEFL & TESOL certified, and a practice built around one belief: training should change how people actually perform, not just how they feel for an afternoon.

I've spent that decade watching brilliant people struggle to communicate under pressure, first-time managers thrown into leadership with no preparation, and subject experts who know everything except how to teach it. That's the gap I built my work to close.

"Learning should not end when the training session ends. It should begin there."
